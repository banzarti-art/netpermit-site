Terms and payments

Effective 23 November 2026.

The short version. NetPermit is free to use as a firewall. Plus and Pro are optional and are bought through Google Play, which handles the payment, the renewal and any refund. You can cancel at any time in Google Play. If a paid plan ends, the firewall and all your rules keep working.

Using NetPermit

NetPermit is provided for your own use on your own devices. You may not use it to monitor or control a device that belongs to someone else without their knowledge, or for anything unlawful.

The app is licensed to you, not sold. You may not copy, resell or redistribute it, except as the law where you live allows.

Plans

There are three plans.

  • Free is the firewall: blocking and allowing apps, new-app handling, Block all, pausing, and blocked activity for the last day. It needs no account and no payment.
  • Plus adds allowing an app for 15 minutes, 30 minutes or 1 hour, and 7 days of blocked activity.
  • Pro adds everything in Plus, allowing an app for any length of time, Wi-Fi-only apps, 30 days of blocked activity, and saving that record to a file.

What each plan includes is listed in the app under Plans. Only features that work in the version you have installed are listed there. Features described as planned are not part of any plan and are not promised.

Prices and payment

Prices are set in Google Play and shown, in your own currency and including any tax Google applies, in the app and in Google Play before you buy. Payment is taken by Google Play using the payment method on your Google account. We never see or store your payment details.

Subscriptions and renewal

If you buy a plan as a subscription, it renews automatically at the end of each period, at the price then shown in Google Play, until you cancel. Google Play charges the renewal to the same payment method.

If a free trial is offered, Google Play shows its length before you start. You are charged when the trial ends unless you cancel before then.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. In the app, open Plans and tap Manage or cancel in Google Play. Or open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

After you cancel, the plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. Then the app returns to Free. Your rules are kept and blocking carries on.

Changing plan

If you are on Plus and choose Pro in the app, Google Play replaces your Plus subscription with Pro and credits the time you had already paid for. To move from Pro to Plus, cancel Pro in Google Play; when the period you paid for ends, open Plans in the app and choose Plus.

Codes

We sometimes give out a code, for example to someone reviewing the app or as a gift. A code opens Plus or Pro at no charge for the time it was made for: 30 days for a reviewer's access code, and a week, a month, a year or no end for a gift code. A code is not a purchase and does not renew. It works once on an installation. A code made for one phone works on that phone only; a code made for any phone stops working if it is not used by the time it was made to expire, usually two days after it was made. Codes are given, never sold, and cannot be exchanged for money. When a code's time ends, the app returns to the plan you had.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Google Play and refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy. You can ask Google for a refund from your order history. If something has gone wrong with a purchase, write to us at netpermit@gmail.com with your Google Play order number and we will help as far as we can. Nothing here limits rights you have under the law where you live.

What NetPermit does and does not promise

NetPermit works through Android's own VPN mechanism and can only do what Android allows. In particular:

  • Android allows one VPN at a time. Another VPN app can stop NetPermit. NetPermit tells you when that happens.
  • While NetPermit is restarting, updating, or paused, apps are not blocked.
  • If Android's "Block connections without VPN" setting is on, Android blocks allowed apps too.
  • A Wi-Fi-only app can use a small amount of mobile data in the moment the phone switches from Wi-Fi to mobile data.
  • NetPermit does not inspect or filter the traffic of apps you allow.

NetPermit is a tool to help you control your phone. It is not a guarantee of security or privacy, and it is provided as it is, without warranties beyond those the law requires. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses that result from using or being unable to use the app.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. A change will not take away something you have already paid for during the period you paid for.
