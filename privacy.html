Privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026. This policy covers the NetPermit app for Android (com.netpermit.app) and this website.

The short version. NetPermit controls which apps on your phone can use the Internet, and it does that on your phone. It has no account, shows no ads, and does not send your data to us or to anyone else. We do not have servers that receive information from the app.

How NetPermit works

NetPermit uses Android's VPN feature (VpnService) to block the apps you have not allowed. Android calls this a VPN and shows a key icon while it is on, but NetPermit is not a VPN service: it does not route your traffic through a remote server and it does not change your IP address.

  • Apps you block have their connection attempts sent to NetPermit on your phone, where they are discarded.
  • Apps you allow connect to the Internet directly. Their traffic does not pass through NetPermit and NetPermit cannot see it.

NetPermit never reads, stores or changes the content of any connection, and it does not decrypt anything.

What NetPermit keeps on your phone

All of this stays on your device.

  • The list of apps on your phone. NetPermit asks Android for the list of every app and system component installed, so that there is a switch for each one. A firewall cannot control what it cannot name. The list is used only to show and apply your rules. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Your rules. Which apps you allowed or blocked, and any time limits.
  • Blocked activity. When a blocked app tries to connect, NetPermit records which app it was and the site name it asked for or the address it aimed at, with a count. This is encrypted on your phone and deleted after 1 day on Free, 7 days on Plus or 30 days on Pro. You can delete it, or switch recording off, in the app at any time.
  • Counts and history. How many connection attempts were blocked, and when protection was on, off or could not be confirmed.
  • App usage time, data used by each app, and which app is open, only if you choose to grant Android's Usage Access permission. It is optional. NetPermit uses it to show how long apps were used and how much data each used (Android's own count, read on the phone when you open Insights), and to ask about a blocked app you are using rather than about every app that tries to connect in the background. None of it is kept: it is read again when it is shown, and which app is open is looked at only when it is needed.
  • Device health and security settings read from Android, for example the battery level or whether a screen lock is set, so the app can show them to you.
  • Which plan you have.

What leaves your phone

Nothing is sent to us. NetPermit has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising identifier and no cloud backup of its data. Android's own backup of app data is switched off for NetPermit. The app itself opens no connection to any server.

Two things involve someone else, and only when you choose them:

  • Buying a plan. Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. NetPermit asks Google Play which plan you have, and learns only that. We do not receive your name, your email address or your payment details from the app.
  • Saving a file. If you choose Export rules, or Save to a file in Blocked activity, NetPermit writes a file to the place you pick. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you send, and use it only to answer you.

This website

These pages use no cookies and no analytics, and load nothing from anywhere else. They are hosted by GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser loads a page, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive that information.

One page runs a few lines of script: the page a gift code's link opens. The script reads the code from the link and offers to open it in NetPermit. It runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. The code is the part of the link after the # sign, which a browser keeps to itself and does not send to this website or to anyone else.

One more page has a script, and it is for NetPermit's publisher, not for visitors: a page for making gift codes on the publisher's own phone. It is not linked from these pages, it does nothing for anyone who opens it without the publisher's key, and it keeps nothing in a visitor's browser. On the publisher's phone it keeps the publisher's own key and list. Like the other, it sends nothing anywhere, and the page itself forbids its browser from connecting to anything.

Children

NetPermit is a general tool and is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone. A parent can set it up on a child's phone ("Child's phone"); then the parent PIN is kept on that phone only, as a one-way hash from which the PIN cannot be read back, and nothing about the child's use of the phone is recorded for anyone or sent anywhere.

Your choices

You can delete the blocked-activity record, switch its recording off, pause or turn off protection, and remove everything by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete or send you on request.
