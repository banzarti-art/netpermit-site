Contact

Questions, problems, or something that doesn't look right: write to us.

Email

netpermit@gmail.com

To help us help you

If you are reporting a problem, these make it much quicker to find:

  • Your phone model and Android version (Settings, About phone).
  • The NetPermit version (in the app: Settings and more, About NetPermit).
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • For a purchase: your Google Play order number, which starts with GPA.

Please don't send passwords or payment card details. We never need them.

Before you write

The guide covers the most common questions, including what to do when an allowed app won't connect and when notifications stop arriving.

Subscriptions and refunds

Subscriptions are managed in Google Play, where you can cancel at any time. Refunds are handled by Google Play. See Terms and payments.
