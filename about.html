About NetPermit

A firewall for your Android phone that tells you the truth about what it is doing.

What it is

NetPermit lets you decide which apps on your phone may use the Internet. Every app has a switch. Apps you install start blocked until you say otherwise.

NetPermit uses Android's VPN feature to do the blocking, so Android shows a key icon and calls it a VPN. It is a firewall that runs on your phone, not a VPN service. Your traffic is not sent to a remote server and your IP address does not change. Apps you block have their connection attempts discarded on the phone. Apps you allow connect directly, exactly as they would without NetPermit, and NetPermit never sees what they send.

What we care about

  • Your phone, your decision. Nothing is allowed behind your back. The parts of the phone that start allowed are listed, labelled, and can be switched off.
  • Say only what is true. NetPermit shows an app as blocked or allowed once Android has confirmed it, and says so plainly when it cannot be sure.
  • Keep it on the phone. No account, no ads, no analytics. We do not run servers that receive anything from the app.
  • Blocking is free. The firewall is free and stays free. Paid plans add convenience, never the protection itself.

What it is not

NetPermit is not an antivirus, a VPN service, a cleaner, or a tool for monitoring someone else's phone. It does not block ads or trackers inside the apps you allow. It cannot see what allowed apps send.

Who makes it

NetPermit is published on Google Play by the developer named on its Google Play listing. You can reach us at netpermit@gmail.com.
